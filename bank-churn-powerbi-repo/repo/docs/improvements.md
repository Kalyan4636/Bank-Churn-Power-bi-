# Ideas to make the dashboard stand out
1. Insight titles ("Germany churns at 2x France") instead of repeated generic titles.
2. KPI row: churned customers, retention rate, avg balance of churned, avg credit score, with targets and arrows.
3. One color story (coral = churn, teal = retained) on every visual.
4. Percent labels on donuts; consider 100% stacked bars for comparisons.
5. Age x Country churn heatmap and a top-5 risk segments table.
6. Key Influencers or Decomposition Tree to show why customers leave.
7. Slicers (Country, Gender, Products), bookmarks, reset button, drill-through customer list, tooltip page, navigation.
8. Action panel with a recommendation for the highest-risk segment.
9. Consistent fonts (Segoe UI), clean number formats (20.4%, 10K), grid alignment.
10. Optional: churn probability model and an "at risk" customer count.
